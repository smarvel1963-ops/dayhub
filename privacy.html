Day Hub privacy policy

Day Hub, Cruise Hub and Trip Hub are apps by Marvel Corp. Last updated October 7, 2026.

The short version

What you type into Day Hub stays on your phone. We have no Day Hub account system and no database of your plans. There are no ads, no ad trackers and no analytics in the app. A few features you choose to turn on send data to other services, and each one is listed below.

What stays on your phone

Your schedule, to-dos, lists, notes, bills, trips, work hours and settings are saved in your browser's storage on your phone. We cannot see them. You can erase them any time in ⚙ Settings → Your data → Erase, and uninstalling the app or clearing its site data also removes them.

Google user data

Day Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the features you turned on, in the app, on your phone. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and no person at Marvel Corp reads it. To disconnect, use ⚙ Settings in Day Hub or remove Day Hub at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The backup copy can be deleted from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

Children

Day Hub is for people 13 and older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Selling data

We do not sell or rent personal information. We hold almost none of it.

Changes

If what Day Hub sends anywhere changes, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.
